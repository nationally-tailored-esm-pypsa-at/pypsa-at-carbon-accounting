# Changelog of the carbon accounting fork

Changes of this fork on top of [PyPSA-AT](https://github.com/AGGM-AG/pypsa-at). Changes of
PyPSA-AT itself are in `CHANGELOG.AT.md`, which stays identical to PyPSA-AT.
Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Switchable carbon accounting method `mods.carbon_accounting.method` (default `technology`, the status quo). With `carrier`, green gas, oil and methanol book zero at production and use, the consuming country claims green certificates up to its own consumption (gas only along physical pipeline flows), and captured CO2 counts as negative emissions in the capturing country unless it is used for e-fuels (the e-fuel plant books the CO2 it uses). The EU-wide total is unchanged; scenario `CA_carrier` in the carbon-accounting config and the evaluation view `view_balance_carbon_claims` compare the methods
- Optional fair share for the carrier-based carbon accounting (`mods.carbon_accounting.fair_share`, default off): pooled oil, kerosene and methanol claims of countries with a national CO2 budget are capped at their share of the EU's fixed demand times the EU green production; scenario `CA_carrier_fair` and a cap group in `view_balance_carbon_claims`
- Marimo notebook `evals/carbon_accounting/notebooks/carbon_accounting.py` (with `evals/carbon_accounting/accounting.py`) comparing carbon accounting runs for one country: consistency checks, a national CO2 balance bridge (physical vs accounting steps, and every step split into system response and accounting method), the cost of the national target with a budget regime, the implicit transfers of green fuel attributes (who receives the value of a green MWh and of the national CO2 price, money through the attribute pools per country), and the physical system response
- Marimo notebook `evals/carbon_accounting/notebooks/carbon_accounting_pathways.py` (with `evals/carbon_accounting/pathways.py`) evaluating every carbon accounting scenario for all horizons under the evaluators `CA_technology` and `CA_carrier`, which read the same network elements and differ only in which categories they count (claims count 0 in technology runs), to compare national totals, budget gaps, the gap of every evaluator to the scenario's own method over all horizons, and decarbonisation pathways in the categories of the national balance bridge
- Reduced configuration `config/config.carbon-accounting.yaml` (AT10DE5, Gurobi) as a fast base for comparing carbon accounting methods; each myopic horizon composes and solves in about 3.5 minutes
- Design-principle effect runs `DP_base`, `DP1_no_national_data`, `DP2_at10` and `DP3_no_national_policy` in their own overlay `config/config.design-principles.yaml` with `config/scenarios.design-principles.yaml` (AT35DE5 except `DP2_at10`, Gurobi; spec in `docs-ca/explanations/design-principle-effects-spec.md`). New switch `mods.carbon_accounting.onwind_brownfield.enable` (default `true`) keeps the PyPSA-Eur onwind capacities in AT when off; a national CO2 budget set to `null` in `solving.constraints.co2_budget_national` adds no constraint for that country. `DP3_no_national_policy` removes all of P3: the national AT targets and the international embedding and national settings (TYNDP NTC floors and flow limits, Russian gas block, gas storage override, capacity trajectories); the TYNDP H2 import scenario and the gas grid freeze are kept
- Design-principle runs `DP3_with_national_policy` and `DP3_no_national_policy` form the P3 pair on the KLIEN technical potentials (`mods.klien_potential_limits.use_technical_potentials: true`, one switch for both), because the KLIEN scenario potentials carry policy assumptions themselves. Replaces the `DP3a_*_no_klien` runs; the pair is compared with each other, not with `DP_base`
- `DP3_no_national_policy` also removes the constraints on cross-border flows and infrastructure: TYNDP NTC flow limits and capacity floors (`mods.tyndp_cross_border_flow_limits`, `mods.tyndp_lower_bounds` off), the DE net electricity import limit (set to a non-binding 500 TWh/a), the TYNDP H2 import generators (`sector.imports.carriers_tyndp: []`) and the frozen gas grid (`mods.threshold_year_for_gas_grid_expansion: 2020`). Config only, no code change
- Marimo notebook `evals/carbon_accounting/notebooks/design_principles.py` (with `evals/carbon_accounting/design_principles.py`) comparing the design-principle runs with `DP_base`: checks (horizons, AT buses per NUTS level, config diff per run, no national policy left in `DP3`), binding global constraints, cost in AT, and one question per principle: potentials and binding potential caps (P1), capacities per NUTS 2 region and gross intra-AT transmission (P2), value, target and dual of every AT policy constraint (re-evaluated on the solved network), the outcomes without them and the constraints that bind the same elements then (P3). Spec in `docs-ca/explanations/design-principle-eval-spec.md`
- Design-principle sensitivity runs (#19): 10 runs `DP1S_*` and `DP3S_*` in `config/scenarios.design-principles.yaml`, listed in the new overlay `config/config.design-principles-sensitivities.yaml` (on top of `config/config.design-principles.yaml`). S1 KLIEN ambition low/high (P1), S2 DP3 split into national targets and international embedding, S3 H2 import price ±50 %, S4 extension limits of the AT cross-border lines and links half/double, S5 EU CO2 cap set to the AT budget path (P3). New switch `mods.carbon_accounting.cross_border_extension_limits` (default: no change) limits the extension of the cross-border AC lines and DC links of some countries on the basis of the upstream limits. Marimo notebook `evals/carbon_accounting/notebooks/design_principle_sensitivities.py` (with `evals/carbon_accounting/sensitivities.py`) shows the effect of each principle at every sensitivity point next to its base effect, the S2 interaction term and the solve status per run and horizon. Spec in `docs-ca/explanations/design-principle-sensitivities-spec.md`
- `fix_unpaired_gas_pipelines` (CA hook before `check_retrofit_pairing`): after `mods.threshold_year_for_gas_grid_expansion`, gas pipelines without an extendable H2 retrofit candidate are fixed, as `fix_gas_grid_capacity` does before it. Fixes the 2030 compose of `DP3_no_national_policy` and the sensitivity runs based on it, where PyPSA-DE still keeps the DE-internal retrofit candidates fixed (Kernnetz); no change in the PyPSA-AT default
- `DP3_no_national_policy` and `DP3S_embedding_only` also set `mods.tyndp_lower_bounds.years: []`: `apply_tyndp_transmission_lower_bounds` checks only the years, not `enable`, and crashed in 2030 and 2040 reading the input the rule drops when the switch is off

### Changed
- The carbon accounting lives in its own paths (#7): code in `mods/carbon_accounting/`, `evals/carbon_accounting/` and `test/test_carbon_accounting/`, defaults in `config/config.ca.yaml` and `config/plotting.ca.yaml` (loaded after the PyPSA-AT config), docs in `docs-ca/`. With `mods.carbon_accounting.enable`, the carbon accounting's own national CO2 budget replaces the PyPSA-AT one; both are equal under the method `technology`
